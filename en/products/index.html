What each line is designed to do.

Each card starts with the need, not the product name, so you can immediately understand the line's role.

Availability may change and the final choice is always personalised in the salon.

For dull or congested skin

DETERSIONE

The first step that changes everything: removes residue, excess oil and surface cells that make skin look dull and less receptive.

  • Cleaner, smoother-feeling skin
  • More even-looking texture
  • Prepares for the next products
For skin that looks less firm

TONIFICAZIONE

Body care for skin that appears relaxed, thin or depleted and needs daily cosmetic support.

  • Supports tone and firmness
  • Helps skin elasticity
  • Ideal in firming programmes
For hormonally changing skin

DONNA+ face

Face care created for peri-menopause, menopause and post-menopause, when skin may become drier, thinner and less elastic.

  • Cosmetic plumping action
  • Supports tone and radiance
  • Comfort for drier skin
For dark spots and uneven tone

GLOWHITE

Brightening and depigmenting care for uneven tone linked to sun, age or hormonal changes, designed to be used with care and consistency.

  • Visually evens the complexion
  • Works on the look of dark spots
  • Helps restore radiance
For hydration and radiance

PETALI DI LUCE

Complete face care for dehydrated, delicate or mature skin, with steps supporting exfoliation, nourishment and renewal.

  • Hydrates and plumps
  • Smooths the skin surface
  • Restores a fresher look
For a targeted response

CROMOAROMA

Not one line but several: purifying for blemishes, blue for sensitive skin, drainage for puffiness and toning for relaxed skin.

  • Face: blemishes or sensitivity
  • Body: drainage or tone
  • Chromotherapy and aromatherapy
For puffiness and heavy legs

NIO-REDUCELL

A leg and abdomen routine supporting work on water retention, cellulite concerns and localised areas between salon treatments.

  • Encourages a feeling of lightness
  • Targets the look of cellulite
  • Scrub, spray oil and targeted cream
For personalised nourishment

ESSENTIAL

Essential oils and massage bases are combined around your needs, with targeted body creams continuing care at home.

  • Four-step detox
  • Nourishing, elasticising or age-supporting care
  • Tailored aromatic experience
When you enjoy perfuming the skin

PROFUMO IN EMULSIONE & AMBER ROMANCE

Creamy textures and solid perfumes applied where you prefer: a small, simple way to perfume the skin throughout the day.

  • Intimate, adjustable scent
  • Easy to carry
  • A sensory gift idea
For rough body skin

SCRUB CORPO

Exfoliation removes surface cells and leaves skin smoother, more even and ready for creams, oils and body treatments.

  • Smooths rough areas
  • Refreshes the look of skin
  • Prepares for body treatments
For daily sun protection

SUN-SATIONAL

Face and body sunscreen selected around phototype, exposure and texture preference—an essential step against uneven tone too.

  • UVA and UVB protection
  • Comfortable, invisible textures
  • Options for sensitive skin
When more technical support is useful

TECNIWORK & TNS

Complementary professional products selected for specific needs and to support at-home maintenance after a salon service.

  • Professionally selected
  • Personalised use instructions
  • Recommended only when useful
